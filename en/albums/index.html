    Frequently asked questions about albums

    How many photos fit in an album?

    An album with 5 spreads looks best with 12 to 15 photos. Each extra spread fits about 3 more photos on average.

    What does an extra spread cost?

    An extra spread costs €10. You can choose up to 12 spreads.

    Can I have a name printed on the album?

    Yes, for a surcharge of €19. You type the text in the configurator and see a preview on the cover straight away.

    Do I have to choose the photos myself?

    No. You can upload your favourites, but you can also let us pick the best photos from your shoot. Then you do not have to upload anything.

    What kind of album is it?

    A hardcover album with a linen cover, in 2 sizes (15 × 20 and 20 × 30 cm) and 17 colours of linen and natural linen. Prices include VAT.

    What happens after my request?

    We will contact you via WhatsApp within 24 hours to go through your choices. A request is not yet a final order.

    Questions about your album?

    Send us a message, call or WhatsApp. We reply within 24 hours.
